---
name: Research before every decision
description: Every phase or decision starts with a research gate run by parallel agents, with sources and a 3-5 year outlook
type: feedback
---

Before choosing a tool, a market, a price or a plan, run a research gate: several agents in parallel, each with one question, each returning claims with URL, date and confidence. Then a short synthesis with the contradictions listed.

**Why:** Decisions made from the model's memory were repeatedly out of date by 6-18 months (pricing pages, platform rules, API names). Ten minutes of research saved weeks of rework.

**How to apply:**
- Write the questions first, one per agent, including "what changes in the next 3-5 years".
- Every claim carries [V] / [M] / [EST] and a source.
- Store the synthesis as a numbered research file and cite it in the plan.
