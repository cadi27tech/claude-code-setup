---
name: Estimates need a confirm gate
description: Numbers the model estimated are marked [EST] and confirmed by the owner before they appear in anything sent
type: feedback
---

Any number the model inferred rather than read from a source is written with an [EST] label. Before a document, proposal or post leaves the machine, every [EST] is either confirmed by the owner or removed.

**Why:** A proposal once quoted a "40% time saving" that came from the model's guess, not from the client project. The client asked for the data. There was none.

**How to apply:**
- Label every figure: [V] verified this session, [M] from notes or memory, [EST] estimate.
- Before anything is sent, list all [EST] items in one message and wait for confirmation.
- Never upgrade [EST] to a plain number by rewording it.
